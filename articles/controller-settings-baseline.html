Controller Settings: A Better Starting Point

Find sensitivity you can control consistently

A sensible baseline helps you improve without constant tweaking.

Start with defaults

Play a few sessions at default values before changing everything. Adjust one axis or setting at a time.

Understand deadzones

A deadzone that is too large may make small movements unresponsive; too small may reveal stick drift.

Balance sensitivity and precision

Higher sensitivity helps turn quickly but can make aiming harder. Test repeatable movements and use the same practice routine.

Save configurations

Record your changes so you can restore earlier settings. Match your setup to the game and your comfort.

Final takeaway

Start with one small change, check whether it works for you, and build on what you learn. Gaming should remain enjoyable and within your budget.
