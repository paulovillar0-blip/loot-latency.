60Hz vs 120Hz for Gaming

Understand refresh rate before upgrading

Higher refresh rates can look smoother, but content and hardware matter.

What refresh rate means

A 60Hz display can update up to 60 times a second; a 120Hz display up to 120 times. More updates may improve perceived motion clarity and responsiveness.

Frame rate is different

Your game and device must produce sufficient frames to benefit fully from a higher-refresh display. Poor frame pacing may still look uneven.

Battery and tradeoffs

On a phone, higher refresh rates can increase battery usage depending on the display and workload. Adaptive refresh may help.

Who benefits most

Fast-paced games are more likely to show a difference than turn-based games. Test a display in person if possible.

Final takeaway

Start with one small change, check whether it works for you, and build on what you learn. Gaming should remain enjoyable and within your budget.
