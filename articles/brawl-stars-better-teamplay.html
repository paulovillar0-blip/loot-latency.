Brawl Stars Teamplay Fundamentals

Improve your positioning and team decisions

Control lanes and objectives rather than chasing every elimination.

Understand your role

Different brawlers thrive at different ranges and on different maps. Consider cover, objectives and your teammates before pushing.

Control lanes patiently

Winning space creates safe angles for allies. Avoid feeding opponents by taking isolated fights without a plan.

Use supers with purpose

Save powerful abilities for moments when they change a fight or secure an objective rather than using them automatically.

Review patterns

After a loss, ask whether positioning, matchup selection or timing could improve. Balance updates may change specific best picks.

Final takeaway

Start with one small change, check whether it works for you, and build on what you learn. Gaming should remain enjoyable and within your budget.
