Performance

Stutter vs Lag: What Is the Difference?

Diagnose the problem before changing settings

Frame-time spikes and network delay require different fixes.

Recognize the symptoms

Stutter feels like uneven animation or brief freezes. Network lag often appears as delayed commands, teleporting characters or rubber-banding.

Track frame times

A game can report high average FPS while still feeling jerky. Watch for long frame-time spikes and investigate background apps, shaders, thermal throttling and storage.

Test the network separately

Look for packet loss, unstable ping and congested Wi-Fi. Test with a local single-player game to separate device issues from online connection problems.

Change one setting at a time

Lower demanding graphics options and cap FPS to a stable rate. Keep a record so you know which change helped.

Final takeaway

Start with one small change, check whether it works for you, and build on what you learn. Gaming should remain enjoyable and within your budget.
