PRIVACY NOTICE.

Plain-language information about what this website does and does not collect.

Last updated: November 13, 2026.

What this site currently does

Loot & Latency is a static website. The guide search, budget planner, and network checklist run locally in your browser. In the starter version, these features do not submit answers, queries, or budget data to a server and do not set analytics or advertising cookies.

Hosting data

The company hosting this website may process normal technical information, such as IP addresses, request times, and user-agent data, to deliver pages, maintain security, and operate infrastructure. The host's own terms and privacy policies apply. We currently use an external hosting provider to deliver the website, and may change providers as the project develops.

External shopping links

Some pages link to external stores. Visiting those sites may cause those services to collect data according to their own privacy policies. We do not control how third-party websites process your information.

Advertising and analytics

There are no advertising scripts or analytics services active in the current version. If the owner later enables Google AdSense, analytics, or other tracking technologies, this notice must be updated to explain the services, cookie use, legal basis and consent choices where required. Any required consent-management mechanism must be added before activating relevant advertising features.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact page when a public editorial contact address has been configured. This page describes our current technical setup and should be reviewed for the laws applicable to the site's visitors.
