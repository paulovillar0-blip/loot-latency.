WHO WE ARE / WHY THIS EXISTS

FOR THE LOVE OF BETTER PLAY.

Loot & Latency is an independent gaming guide project built around a simple idea: players deserve clear advice, without exaggerated claims or manufactured urgency.

OUR PHILOSOPHY
✳

Useful beats loud.

We cover practical performance fixes, thoughtful gear buying decisions, and the things that can make games more enjoyable on PC, console, and mobile.

Our guides are educational. We don't claim to have personally tested products unless we have actually done so. We don't post invented benchmarks, fabricated reviews, or guaranteed fixes. When a recommendation depends on your equipment, location, or game, we say so.
